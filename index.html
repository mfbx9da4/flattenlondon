      How this works

      Routes are computed in your browser over about 1.4 million street segments, using Environment Agency 1 m LIDAR (composite DTM) elevation and the Overture / OpenStreetMap street graph. The slider runs along the whole trade-off between distance and climbing: every route that nothing else beats on both counts, from the shortest path to the flattest one worth walking (where a metre of climb costs 200 m of walking; beyond that the routes stop being routes). Sliding right never shortens the route and never adds climbing. Climbing is cumulative gain, not the net difference between the endpoints. Steps are allowed on foot; on a bike, steps and footpaths without cycle access are left out. Faint lines show the other routes along the way. Place search is offline: street junctions, places and postcodes for Greater London load into this page. Source and data.
